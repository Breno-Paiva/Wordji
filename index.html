Game Modes

Vocabulário
  • Translate everyday words between English and Portuguese.
  • A word appears — type the translation, one letter box per letter.
  • Press Space or Enter to submit.
  • You have 3 tries to get each word right.
  • Press Tab or hit Skip to pass on a word.
  • Score as many as you can before time runs out.
Verbos
  • Conjugate Portuguese verbs across pronouns and tenses you choose.
  • You'll see a pronoun, a verb, and a tense — type the conjugated form in the single wide box.
  • Optionally translate the verb's English gloss first, before conjugating it.
  • Press Space or Enter to submit.
  • You have 3 tries to get each one right.
  • Press Tab or hit Skip to pass.
  • Score as many as you can before time runs out.
Round Length
  • ⏱️ Timed — 10, 30 or 60 seconds. The clock counts down.
  • 🔢 Words — a fixed 10, 25 or 50 wordjis. The clock counts up.
  • ♾️ Unlimited — no clock, no cap. Quit when you're done.
Quitting
  • Quit (or Esc) ends the round early and takes you to your score.
  • What you scored still counts — quitting can only ever lower it.
